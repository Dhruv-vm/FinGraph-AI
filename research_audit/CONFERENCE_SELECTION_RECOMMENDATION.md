# FinGraph AI — Top 3 Recommended Conference Targets & Action Plans

**Audit Date:** 2026-11-01  
**Specialist:** Academic Advisory Panel  

---

## 1. Primary Recommendation (Target A): IEEE BigData 2027 / Regular Research Track
- **Why It Fits:**
  IEEE BigData specifically emphasizes enterprise-scale Knowledge Graphs, hybrid retrieval systems, and big data applications in industry and finance. FinGraph AI's 10,054 nodes, 8,181 chunks, and bitemporal point-in-time snapshotting match the technical scope of the Big Data Search & Mining track perfectly.
- **Official Template:** Standard IEEE double-column format (`IEEEtran.cls`, 10 pt font).
- **Page Limits:** 10 pages inclusive of all figures, tables, and algorithms + 2 pages of references.
- **Missing Requirements to Satisfy Reviewers:**
  1. Dense vector-only baseline and Graph-only baseline tables (EXP-1 and EXP-2).
  2. Latency and computational profile table (EXP-7).
- **Publication & Indexing:** Published in IEEE Xplore, fully indexed in Scopus, Web of Science, and DBLP. Highly respected CORE B venue.

---

## 2. Domain-Specific Recommendation (Target B): ACM ICAIF (AI in Finance)
- **Why It Fits:**
  The ACM International Conference on AI in Finance is the premier specialized global venue organized by ACM SIGAI. The reviewer pool consists of Wall Street quant researchers, central bank data scientists, and financial NLP academics who directly understand SEC Form 10-K disclosures, bitemporal availability, and the criticality of zero-hallucination refusal guarantees.
- **Official Template:** ACM Primary Article Template (`acmart.cls`, `sigconf` style).
- **Page Limits:** 8 pages + unlimited references.
- **Missing Requirements to Satisfy Reviewers:**
  1. Deep dive on entity-balanced comparative queries (CDC series).
  2. Brief discussion linking SEC disclosures with the auxiliary walk-forward return forecasting experiment in Section IX.
- **Publication & Indexing:** Published in ACM Digital Library, indexed in Scopus, DBLP, and ACM SIGAI.

---

## 3. Pragmatic / Rapid Recommendation (Target C): FinNLP Workshop @ EMNLP or IEEE ICTAI
- **Why It Fits:**
  - **Option C1 (FinNLP Workshop at EMNLP):** Provides direct affiliation with EMNLP (CORE A*). Acceptance rate is higher for empirical system demonstrations (~30–35%), reviews are provided by leading financial NLP scholars, and papers appear in the ACL Anthology and Scopus.
  - **Option C2 (IEEE ICTAI):** Long-running CORE B venue with high reliability, rapid review turnaround, and IEEE Xplore publication.
- **Action Plan:** Prepare the manuscript under the IEEE 8–10 page template. If targeting ACM ICAIF, reformat with `acmart.cls`.
